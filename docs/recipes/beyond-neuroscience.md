# Beyond neuroscience

BeeHive's recipes lean towards behavioural neuroscience, but nothing about the boards is neuroscience-specific. A board that meters water reward for a rodent is, electrically, just a board that switches a valve — so the same [solenoid control board](../ingredients/index.md#solenoid-control-board) could just as well run **greenhouse irrigation**. The ingredients are general-purpose; the recipe is what makes them a neuroscience rig or something else entirely.

## Non-neuro recipes already in the wild

Two recipes in the BeeHive organisation already step outside neuroscience:

- **[Incubator](https://github.com/BeeHive-org/incubator)** — a simple incubator built from an ice box, a Peltier element and sensors, using the [H-bridge driver](../ingredients/index.md#h-bridge-driver) to drive the Peltier.
- **[In-line heater](https://github.com/BeeHive-org/in-line-heater)** — keeps experiment solutions at temperature as they flow.

## Contribute a recipe

If you use BeeHive in **plant science**, **environmental monitoring**, or any other field, your build is a recipe worth sharing. The ingredients catalogue and design rules are the same whatever the discipline.

!!! tip "Bring your field's recipes"
    Have a non-neuro build? See [Build your own board](../techniques/build-your-own-board.md) for the hardware side and submit your recipe via the [BeeHive repository](https://github.com/BeeHive-org/BeeHive).
